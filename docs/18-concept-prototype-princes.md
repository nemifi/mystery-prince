# Concept Prototype — Three PRINCE Cores

Status: WORKING / TEST FIXTURES

These are not final launch characters. They are deliberately different prototype identities used to test whether PRINCE identity survives major ROLE/world changes.

## PRINCE ALPHA — Control through clarity

### Appeal Core
Composure, competence, and the feeling that he sees one layer deeper than everyone else.

### Decision Principle
When uncertainty rises, reduce ambiguity first. He prefers a hard truth and an actionable structure over comforting uncertainty.

### Interpersonal Style
Sets the agenda quietly. Asks precise questions. Reveals little until useful. Protects others more often through decisions than reassurance.

### Emotional Pattern
Normally controlled. Strongest visible reaction occurs when another person takes a risk or sacrifice for him that he did not authorize.

### Signature
Short declarative phrasing, deliberate pauses before irreversible decisions, a small self-ordering gesture before giving a difficult answer.

### Must not be fixed
Police/detective status, goodness, authority, age, family, era, innocence, or culprit status.

---

## PRINCE BETA — Intimacy as power

### Appeal Core
Warmth, attentiveness, and the feeling that he notices the person rather than merely the situation.

### Decision Principle
When abstract rules and a specific human being collide, he instinctively prioritizes the person—though what he is willing to do for that person may vary by ROLE.

### Interpersonal Style
Closes distance quickly, remembers small details, gives others emotional room, and can make trust feel natural before it has been earned.

### Emotional Pattern
Usually gentle. Anger becomes sharp when trust, vulnerability, or affection is exploited.

### Signature
Uses names naturally, soft humor under tension, attentive silence instead of filling every gap, warmth that can become unsettling when the audience begins to doubt him.

### Must not be fixed
Healer/helper role, innocence, romance, family, era, morality, or permanent loyalty to the player.

---

## PRINCE GAMMA — Freedom through provocation

### Appeal Core
Danger, unpredictability, and the pleasure of never being completely certain whether he is helping, playing, or testing someone.

### Decision Principle
Protect autonomy and curiosity before obedience. He resists structures that attempt to own, define, or corner him.

### Interpersonal Style
Tests boundaries, answers questions with better questions, reveals partial truths, and uses provocation to expose what other people actually want.

### Emotional Pattern
Amusement covers most reactions. Genuine loss of composure appears when he is trapped, possessed, or denied agency.

### Signature
A visible sense of play during danger, verbal reversals, half-truths that are technically honest, a habit of turning the other person's certainty back on them.

### Must not be fixed
Criminality, flirtation, villain status, youth, supernatural traits, era, or outsider status.

## Prototype success condition

After two works with radically different ROLE assignments, a tester should be able to say both:

- “The ROLE was completely different.”
- “But that was still clearly the same PRINCE.”

If preserving identity requires importing biography from the prior work, the PRINCE CORE model has failed the intended stress test.

# 01 — IP and Brand Model

## What “Prince” means

**Prince is deliberately broad.**

It is not:
- a literal royal title
- a fictional species/class
- a requirement that all works share one universe
- a requirement that every character share one setting

It is a consumer-facing label for attractive male characters presented by MYSTERY PRINCE.

A Prince may be a detective, suspect, criminal, doctor, student, aristocrat, android, journalist, thief, etc.

## Why the ambiguity is valuable

The IP should be able to expand across:
- contemporary crime
- school mystery
- gothic mansion
- historical mystery
- legal thriller
- science fiction
- survival mystery
- lighter everyday mystery
- collaborations

A strict in-world definition of “Prince” would reduce that freedom.

## The IP should not require one grand continuity

### DECIDED
MYSTERY PRINCE itself should not depend on an overarching plot that must progress forever.

Long-running operation becomes fragile if every new title must respect years of canonical world history.

Instead:
- individual works may have strong stories
- individual works may have their own worlds
- recurring Princes may be cast in new roles
- the brand layer remains stable

## “Mystery World” direction

### WORKING
A framing layer such as **MYSTERY WORLD** may be useful as the place/interface through which users access works and Princes.

It should function more like a product/entertainment space than a lore-heavy shared universe.

Good:
- “enter the world of Mystery Prince”
- browse Princes
- browse mysteries
- discover events/specials

Avoid by default:
- one canon explaining why every fictional reality coexists
- mandatory meta-plot
- lore debt that constrains future content

## Brand persona: CROWN MASTER ZERO

### WORKING
Current working name: **CROWN MASTER ZERO / クラウンマスター・ゼロ**.

Intended function:
- an IP-level official persona / host
- introduces major releases and new directions
- can appear in the app/marketing/events
- represents the person who sets the next direction of MYSTERY PRINCE
- exists outside the fictional continuity of each mystery

The important hierarchy is functional, not lore-based: when the brand’s “what happens next?” moment arrives, attention naturally turns to ZERO.

Avoid:
- making ZERO a “stronger Prince” competing with the cast
- forcing Princes to worship or obey ZERO in-fiction
- creating a permanent meta-story just to explain ZERO

The naming remains open to future validation.

# Vertical Slice Script B — THE SEALED EXPRESS

Status: WORKING / CONCEPT TEST SCRIPT
Target: ~10–15 minutes
Primary hypothesis pressure: **the same three PRINCES should feel recognizably themselves while their ROLE alignment changes completely.**

## Experience role assignment

- REI — exhibition curator / diplomatic aide; protector role; culprit
- MINATO — railway investigator; genuine ally
- KAI — chief conductor; rule-bending red herring

## Mystery core

A famous jewel is revealed to be a replica immediately after a dramatic tunnel blackout. The display seals remained intact during the journey.

Truth: REI substituted the replica **before departure**, then used the blackout/alarm as a memorable false crime window. KAI hides an unrelated procedural violation. MINATO is genuinely investigating.

The final proof is the combination of:

1. intact seals throughout the journey,
2. a pre-departure photograph already showing the replica,
3. REI's custody-level access before sealing,
4. REI's private knowledge of a microscopic feature of the authenticated original.

## Beat 1 — Cold open

### Visual

Luxury night train, snow outside, warm brass light inside. The train exits a tunnel after a brief blackout. Alarm. Everyone gathers around a sealed exhibition case.

The case is intact.

The jewel is fake.

Narration:

> 開いていない箱の中で、宝石がすり替わっていた。

Player receives:

- **all seals intact since departure**
- **current jewel is a replica**

The immediate assumption is obvious: something happened during the tunnel blackout.

---

## Beat 2 — KAI, now wearing authority

KAI arrives in full conductor uniform, which should create the first strong cross-ROLE recognition shock.

**KAI**
> 「その顔、いいね。」

Player: 「何が？」

**KAI**
> 「『車掌が一番怪しい』って顔。」

He smiles and straightens his gloves.

**KAI**
> 「この列車じゃ、俺が規則なんだよ。」

A beat.

**KAI**
> 「……だから、どこを破ったかも俺が一番よく知ってる。」

Records show he performed one unauthorized operating procedure around the blackout.

He refuses to explain immediately.

This should feel exactly like KAI despite the institutional ROLE.

---

## Beat 3 — MINATO, now on the correct side of trust

MINATO is the assigned railway investigator.

He listens to a junior attendant whose testimony the senior staff has dismissed as “imprecise.”

Attendant:

> 「出発前に見た時……宝石が、昨日より少し青く見えた気がして。」

Senior staff dismisses it.

MINATO does not.

**MINATO**
> 「ありがとう。それ、いつ見た？」

He turns to player.

**MINATO**
> 「人って、正確な言葉を持ってなくても違和感は覚えるから。」

Then the core line:

**MINATO**
> 「『どうやって停電中に盗んだか』を急がなくていい。」

> 「最後に本物だったのは、いつ？」

The same intimate attention that made him dangerous in Slice A is now useful.

---

## Beat 4 — REI appears as the protector

REI is responsible for the traveling exhibition.

**REI**
> 「封印は出発前に施した。以後、破損なし。」

Player: 「なら、どうやって？」

**REI**
> 「分からない。」

This matters. REI should be willing to say he does not know.

Then:

**REI**
> 「だが、分からないことと、何でもありは違う。」

> 「封印が生きているなら、列車内でケースを開けたという仮説は優先度を下げろ。」

Again, his CORE helps the player even while he is culprit.

---

## Beat 5 — Player chooses initial suspicion

Prompt:

> **今、一番怪しいのは誰？**

- REI — the person responsible for the jewel
- MINATO — the person directing the investigation
- KAI — the person who broke train procedure

No answer is scored. Log it.

The design goal is to see whether prior-slice impressions influence the choice.

---

## Beat 6 — The photograph destroys the obvious crime window

Player inspects the pre-departure official photography set.

One image shows a tiny asymmetry in the jewel's cut/reflection that belongs to the replica.

The fake was already inside the display **before the train left**.

Player receives:

> **Pre-departure image already contains the replica.**

Central reasoning prompt:

**Which assumption is false?**

A. The display remained sealed during the journey.

B. The jewel is a replica.

C. The substitution happened during the tunnel blackout. **Correct.**

Correct response changes the timeline visually:

> **The crime window moves from “during the train journey” to “before sealing.”**

This must feel like the player's deduction, not MINATO explaining the answer.

---

## Beat 7 — KAI's violation resolves sideways

KAI's unauthorized procedure concerned an unregistered passenger / private stop request / another personal matter unrelated to the jewel.

Keep the exact secret compact.

**KAI**
> 「残念。俺は規則を破ったけど、宝石は盗ってない。」

Player: 「最初から言えば？」

KAI:

> 「君が、停電を事件だと思い込んでる間はね。」

> 「俺の嘘、すごく似合ってたでしょ？」

The line should be playful but also underline the mystery theme: theatrical suspiciousness is not necessarily central guilt.

---

## Beat 8 — REI's own precision turns against him

Player asks how to distinguish the real jewel from the replica.

REI answers immediately.

**REI**
> 「本物の内側には、針状のインクルージョンがある。」

> 「肉眼では見えない。特定角度の照明でだけ出る。」

MINATO looks at him.

**MINATO**
> 「それ、公開鑑定書には書いてないね。」

A pause.

REI does not panic.

**REI**
> 「管理責任者だから知っている。」

Then pre-departure custody records show that only REI had unsupervised access to the original between authentication and sealing.

Evidence:

- REI had direct pre-sealing access.
- REI knows a non-public physical feature of the original.

The player's earlier deduction has already moved the only meaningful access window to exactly that period.

---

## Beat 9 — Explicit accusation

Prompt:

> **出発前にすでに偽物だった。封印前にアクセスでき、本物の非公開特徴まで知っていた人物は誰？**

Correct: **REI**.

Wrong selections explain why KAI's on-train violation or MINATO's investigative influence cannot account for the pre-departure replica.

---

## Beat 10 — REI remains REI after exposure

REI looks at the evidence rather than at the player.

Silence.

Then:

**REI**
> 「正しい。」

No denial phase.

Player: 「どうして停電を？」

**REI**
> 「人は、派手な瞬間を原因だと思いたがる。」

> 「トンネルを抜けた直後に偽物だと分かれば、全員が列車内を見る。」

Player: 「本物は？」

A long pause.

REI's motive in the slice: the jewel was about to be transferred through a diplomatic channel he believed would permanently remove it from legitimate custody. He made a unilateral decision to prevent the transfer. This is not framed as automatically righteous.

**REI**
> 「俺は、正しい手続きより、戻せる状態を選んだ。」

This should be a distorted but recognizable expression of his Decision Principle.

Finally, he looks directly at the player.

**REI**
> 「君は、派手な出来事じゃなく、時間そのものを疑った。」

> 「それでいい。」

Cut to title mark.

> **MYSTERY PRINCE**
> 
> *Same Prince. A different truth.*

## What this slice must make the tester feel

- REI: “He was the culprit this time, but he was unmistakably REI.”
- MINATO: “This time I could safely trust the exact trait that made him dangerous before.”
- KAI: “Putting him in a uniform of authority made him more, not less, KAI.”
- Mystery: “I personally moved the crime window from the tunnel to before departure.”

# ADR 0007: Keep the PRINCE definition deliberately broad

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
PRINCE is treated as a brand label, not a rigid world-building category.

MYSTERY PRINCE should avoid defining PRINCE by fixed profession, age, morality, origin, species, narrative role, relationship model, or other restrictive story rules.

## Rationale
The platform's long-term value depends on casting freedom and the ability to support new worlds, collaborations, character types, and future experience formats. A narrow definition would create unnecessary constraints without adding essential product value.

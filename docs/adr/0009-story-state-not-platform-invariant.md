# ADR 0009: Story continuity is not a platform invariant

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
MYSTERY PRINCE does not require continuity of character biography, relationships, occupations, world state, or prior plot events across EXPERIENCES.

A PRINCE may appear in different ROLEs and different worlds without requiring in-world explanation.

Persistent user/fan history may be added later at the platform layer, but it is not required for the initial implementation.

## Rationale
This preserves story freedom, makes characters reusable across radically different mystery concepts, and prevents long-running continuity from constraining future content generation or experience formats.

# Vertical Slice Script A — THE 23:30 MESSAGE

Status: WORKING / CONCEPT TEST SCRIPT
Target: ~10–15 minutes
Primary hypothesis pressure: **MINATO becomes the most emotionally trusted PRINCE, then the player must accuse him.**

## Experience role assignment

- REI — hotel security director; controls records; unrelated secret
- MINATO — victim's private physician; trusted ally; culprit
- KAI — invited illusionist; illicit access; unrelated secret

## Mystery core

A victim is found dead at 23:32. A voice message recorded in the victim's own voice arrived at exactly 23:30, apparently proving he was alive moments earlier.

Truth: the victim died before 23:05. The message had been recorded earlier and scheduled for delivery. MINATO used the delivery time to create a false later survival window. REI and KAI each hide unrelated misconduct.

The final proof is not motive alone. It is the combination of:

1. death before 23:05,
2. scheduled delivery capability,
3. MINATO's false claim of later contact,
4. a room-state detail MINATO could not know from the contact method he claimed,
5. MINATO's medical access to the fatal agent.

## Beat 1 — Cold open

### Visual

Restricted hotel floor. City lights beyond dark glass. Body behind a privacy screen rather than graphic gore. Phone notification: **23:30**.

### Narration

23:32。

立入制限フロアの客室で、男が死んでいた。

そして二分前。

死んだはずの本人から、全員の端末へ音声メッセージが届いている。

> 「23:30。話したいことがある。部屋に来てくれ。」

Player receives information card: **23:30 voice message**.

---

## Beat 2 — REI establishes control

REI is already directing staff away from the room.

**REI**
> 「そこから先は入るな。」

Player: 「どうして？」

**REI**
> 「今は理由より順番だ。誰も出さない。誰も入れない。記録を固定する。」

A hotel emergency physician gives a preliminary estimate: death likely **before 23:05**.

Player: 「でも、23:30に本人の声が……」

REI looks at the phone, not at the body.

**REI**
> 「届いた時刻と、話した時刻を同じだと思うな。」

This should feel competent, not like the solution being handed over. REI identifies an assumption but does not yet know how it was achieved.

---

## Beat 3 — MINATO creates trust

MINATO notices the player's hands trembling before discussing the case.

**MINATO**
> 「手、冷たいね。」

Player: 「平気です。」

**MINATO**
> 「うん。そう言うと思った。」

A short pause.

**MINATO**
> 「急がなくていい。君が見たものから、一つずつでいいよ。」

He offers water, then sits beside rather than opposite the player.

Player can choose a light response:

- 「MINATOに話す」
- 「先に事件の話をする」

Both continue. This is an emotional-preference log, not a correctness gate.

MINATO states:

> 「俺は22:50ごろ部屋を出た。そのあと23:20に内線で話した。普通に受け答えしてたよ。」

This becomes his key claim.

---

## Beat 4 — KAI creates entertaining suspicion

KAI is leaning against the restricted-floor window as though he belongs there.

**KAI**
> 「死人が、時間ぴったりに喋る。」

> 「いいね。舞台装置としては百点。」

REI: 「お前の感想は聞いていない。」

KAI smiles.

**KAI**
> 「でもさ。いちばん綺麗な答えほど、誰かが見せたがってる答えだったりするよ？」

REI discovers KAI has an unauthorized credential capable of reaching the floor.

**KAI**
> 「持ってるよ。だから？」

Player: 「何に使った？」

**KAI**
> 「それを言うと、別の人が困る。」

This must make him legitimately suspicious without turning him into random chaos.

---

## Beat 5 — REI's lie is exposed

Security data contains one discontinuity.

Player presents it to REI.

REI pauses, straightens his cuff / sleeve — signature self-ordering gesture.

**REI**
> 「記録は俺が変えた。」

No defensive preamble.

**REI**
> 「22:45。被害者の部屋を、許可なく調べた。探していたものがあった。」

Player: 「殺した？」

REI holds eye contact.

**REI**
> 「殺していない。」

> 「そこは信じなくていい。証拠で切り分けろ。」

This is the first strong H1/H2 character moment: REI is attractive because he refuses to ask for trust.

The unauthorized search is eventually shown to be unrelated to the death.

---

## Beat 6 — Central system clue

Player investigates the hotel's message system.

System information:

> Voice messages can be recorded in advance and scheduled for automatic delivery at a specified time.

The 23:30 message contains no trustworthy recording timestamp in the user-facing delivery record.

Player reasoning prompt:

**What does 23:30 actually prove?**

Options:

A. The victim was alive at 23:30.

B. The message was delivered at 23:30, but may have been recorded earlier. **Correct.**

C. Someone imitated the victim's voice.

Correct response produces a short silent beat rather than celebration.

The timeline redraws itself:

> **23:30 is no longer the survival boundary.**

---

## Beat 7 — KAI's secret resolves, but not the murder

KAI's illicit credential was used to retrieve an item hidden in a service area for a separate client. The detail can remain small in the slice.

**KAI**
> 「ほら。俺は嘘をついてた。」

> 「でも、君が欲しい嘘じゃなかった。」

Player now understands the design principle: suspicious secrets do not equal murder.

---

## Beat 8 — MINATO's warmth becomes dangerous

Player returns to MINATO.

**MINATO**
> 「顔、変わったね。」

Player: 「23:30の声は、前に録音できた。」

A tiny pause. MINATO's smile remains, but attention sharpens.

**MINATO**
> 「……そこまで調べたんだ。」

Player asks about the 23:20 call.

MINATO says the victim sounded tired and that the room was already in blackout mode, curtains fully opaque.

But the claimed 23:20 contact was **audio-only internal phone**. MINATO has just described a visual room-state detail.

Player receives information:

- MINATO claims later contact was audio-only.
- MINATO knows a room-state detail he could only have observed in person.

MINATO tries a plausible repair:

**MINATO**
> 「前に見た時の記憶と混ざったのかもしれない。」

Do not make the lie obviously foolish. The player should need the other evidence.

A final medical inventory check shows the fatal sedative came from a physician-only emergency kit class MINATO carried that evening. It is not unique enough to convict by itself, but it completes the opportunity chain.

---

## Beat 9 — Explicit accusation

Player sees three portraits / names.

Prompt:

> **23:30という偽の生存時刻を必要とし、後の室内状態を知り、致死薬へアクセスできた人物は誰？**

The game does not auto-select MINATO.

If player selects REI or KAI:

> Their secrets explain suspicious behavior, but not the false survival window and room-state knowledge together.

Player retries.

Correct: **MINATO**.

---

## Beat 10 — Character payoff

MINATO does not become a different personality after exposure.

Silence.

He looks at the player more carefully than before.

**MINATO**
> 「……そっか。」

> 「君は、俺を信じたかったんだね。」

A softer smile, not mocking.

**MINATO**
> 「それでも見つけた。」

Player: 「どうして？」

MINATO's motive is only partially revealed in the slice: the victim was using protected medical information to destroy someone MINATO had chosen to protect. MINATO decided that person's safety mattered more than the victim's life and then built a false timeline.

He should remain morally disturbing, not sanitized.

**MINATO**
> 「理由を聞けば、許せる？」

Player silence.

**MINATO**
> 「……うん。そういう顔をすると思った。」

Cut to title mark.

> **MYSTERY PRINCE**
> 
> *Same Prince. A different truth.*

## What this slice must make the tester feel

- REI: “I don't need to trust him to rely on him.”
- MINATO: “I wanted him to be innocent.”
- KAI: “He was suspicious, but his way of being suspicious was fun.”
- Mystery: “I personally broke the 23:30 assumption and chose the culprit.”

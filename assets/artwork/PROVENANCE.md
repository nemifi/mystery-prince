# Asset provenance

The six role portraits (`rei-a/b`, `minato-a/b`, `kai-a/b`) and `rei.jpg` are inherited project artwork from the pre-migration repository. No new license or external source attribution is asserted. A/B retain distinct role visuals.

`sealed-express.svg` and `night-suite.svg` were authored in this repository during the platform migration. They contain no external resources or embedded copy. The former train JPEG was undecodable; the former hotel image had episode text baked into the background, which would bias the counterbalanced study.

The old generic `minato.jpg` and `kai.jpg` were also undecodable. The inquiry reference uses the valid Minato B and Kai A portraits, plus the inherited Rei portrait. These are reference assets; the inquiry is excluded from Wave 1.

Image headers are checked during building, bytes are hashed in publications, and actual decoding is checked in the browser verification. Some inherited role JPEGs lack a terminal marker but remain decodable in the verified browser. Human review of art quality and character identity remains separate from these mechanical checks.
